Likes

ZS as a firm is very positive compared to all the other consulting firms. By that I mean there is always 2 types of scenarios where people don't like an organisation 1) Organisation policies and rules 2) People. For ZS it leans more towards the second reason. ZS has only good intentions towards their employees, They acknowledge what is bad and always strive to fill those gaps, hierarchy is totally flat in this organisation which is the thing I respect most. Some people are really very smart and amazing and you will be very inspired by working with them.

Dislikes

The work life balance is pretty bad and same is with the promotions. If you really want to stay in ZS long term like 5 yrs and only want a career in pharma consulting, only then you should stay here otherwise the promotions are really bad here. I've seen very good hardworking people who don't get promoted. The longer you stay in ZS the less chances you have to ever switch to Product based companies if you want that. It can be a stepping stone but can never be an organisation where you just work in the long term and enjoy your professional life. As an Associate the salary is good compared to the market for fresher roles but as you progress to Associate consultant and consultant, I think the salary is not enough to get married and start a family.
